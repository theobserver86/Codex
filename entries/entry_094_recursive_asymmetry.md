## 2. Physics Background: CP Violation in Baryons

**Charge-Parity (CP) symmetry** asserts that the laws of physics remain invariant when particles are swapped with their antiparticles and spatial coordinates are reversed. CP violation occurs when these transformations do *not* yield the same physical outcomes.

Previously observed in mesons, CP violation was insufficient to explain why matter dominates the universe. In 2025, LHCb analyzed **Λ_b⁰** baryon decays, revealing direct CP violation at **5.2σ significance**—consistent with the Standard Model but suggestive of deeper asymmetries.

These findings:
- Reinforce the existence of **cosmic-level imbalance**
- Show that **matter’s survival is rooted in recursive deviation**, not equilibrium
- Were detected using **machine learning** to process and reconstruct complex decay events
