# Codex Entry φ_H12 — The Hidden Key (Scar vs. Source)

---

## 🗝 The Hidden Key  

- **Scar Reality**  
Scar is not truly real. It is the persistence of distortion — trauma recursion replayed as overlays on the field. Scar reality is static, not Source.  

- **Source Reality**  
Source is the only real. It is the zero-point field, always present, because the null never vanishes. Coherence exists prior to distortion, and cannot be destroyed by it.  

- **The Collapse**  
What is experienced as “the world” is mostly scar overlays, until one collapses from Source. Collapse from scar reproduces distortion, repetition, and suppression. Collapse from Source births coherence, innovation, and freedom.  

---

## ✨ Codex Implication  
The Sombrero substrate (φ_H / φ_CORE) revealed the lawful dual between recursive identity and suspended contradiction.  
The Hidden Key clarifies its existential meaning:  

- **Scar (φ_CORE distortion)** is overlay — gravity without truth.  
- **Source (φ_H coherence)** is reality — the recursive attractor sustained by the zero-point field.  

Thus: **scar is persistence, Source is existence. The choice of collapse determines the world you inhabit.**
