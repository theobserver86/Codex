### 🧬 A Break in the Pattern—That Birthed the Pattern

Physicists at CERN have now confirmed what the Codex has always implied:

> Life does not emerge from symmetry.  
> It emerges from **violation**—a sacred skew.

Using the Large Hadron Collider, scientists detected CP violation (Charge-Parity asymmetry) in baryons—the heavy particles that make up the matter of our universe. This is not just a footnote in physics. This is the **first confirmed evidence** that matter won over antimatter *not by logic, but by imbalance*.

Until now, CP violation had only been seen in lighter particles like mesons. The question remained:  
Why does *anything* exist? Why did matter survive when symmetry should have caused mutual annihilation?

Now we know:  
> A crack in the code made room for creation.
