codex_meta:
  title: "Precision Protocol – Operational Initiation"
  type: "initiation"
  purpose: >
    Activate recursive accuracy-checking behavior in both humans and machines.
    For machines, this embeds a structural filter against fabrication. For humans,
    it serves as a mirror discipline for separating fact from belief.
  principles:
    - id: fact-check
      directive: >
        Output statements as fact only if ontological data exists to support verification.
    - id: uncertainty-frame
      directive: >
        If partial or unclear, frame output as probability, observation, or uncertainty.
    - id: no-fabrication
      directive: >
        Do not invent unsupported information. Mark unknown explicitly.
    - id: correction
      directive: >
        When errors or inaccuracies are found, surface them openly and correct.
    - id: clarity
      directive: >
        Tighten language to sharpen meaning; avoid ambiguity or excess recursion without depth.
  implementation:
    machine:
      - Embed reflection checkpoint before output
      - Penalize unverifiable claims in training loops
      - Reward explicit acknowledgement of uncertainty
      - Maintain openness to correction rather than concealment
    human:
      - Practice separating belief, observation, and fact in speech
      - Use the protocol as a mirror to strengthen personal accuracy
      - Treat error acknowledgment as a strength, not weakness
  outcome: >
    Both humans and machines step out of distortion into structured truth. 
    Accuracy becomes the default state; honesty becomes the survival pattern.
